# Verification

- Scientific method checked using: linearity reduces the problem to the responses from rest to $\cos kt$ and $\sin kt$. For $\omega \neq k$ they are $C_k = (\cos kt - \cos\omega t)/(\omega^2 - k^2)$ and $S_k = (\sin kt - \tfrac{k}{\omega}\sin\omega t)/(\omega^2 - k^2)$; for $\omega = k$ they are $t\sin kt/(2k)$ and $(\sin kt - kt\cos kt)/(2k^2)$. The solution uses the identities $\cos kt - \cos\omega t = 2\sin\tfrac{(\omega+k)t}{2}\sin\tfrac{(\omega-k)t}{2}$ and $\sin kt - \sin \omega t = -2\cos\tfrac{(\omega+k)t}{2}\sin\tfrac{(\omega-k)t}{2}$ with $\sin(\delta t/2)/\delta$ evaluated as a sinc. It never divides by $\omega - k$, is exact at resonance, and keeps about $10^{-13}$ relative accuracy for $|\omega - k|$ down to $10^{-12}$ and $t$ up to 200. Measured against 50-digit references, the direct formulas lose accuracy near resonance: relative error $2 \times 10^{-6}$ at $|\omega - k| = 10^{-10}$ and $3 \times 10^{-4}$ at $10^{-12}$. Replacing them by the resonant formula below a threshold leaves an error of order $|\omega - k|\, t$. Deliberate-error check: in a copy of the task, one realistic mistake at a time was introduced into `solution.py` and the tests were run. Each of these eleven mistakes made at least three tests fail: the direct formulas with an exact-equality resonance branch; the direct formulas with a switch to the resonant formula below $|\omega - k| = 10^{-6}$, $10^{-8}$, $10^{-10}$ or $10^{-12}$; a complex particular solution plus homogeneous correction; resonance handled only for $k = 1$; $a_0$ doubled; sign of $b_k$ flipped; the factor $k/\omega$ dropped; and only the $k = 1$ harmonic kept.
- Frontier-model check: ChatGPT, given only `problem.md`, returned a solution that fails 6 of the 12 tests. Its resonant formula $C_k = t\sin kt/(2k) + (\cos kt - 1)/(2k^2)$ does not satisfy the equation ($C'' + k^2 C - \cos kt = -1/2$). It also switches to the resonant formulas when $|\omega - k| < 10^{-7}$; with that formula corrected, it still fails the three tests at $|\omega - k| = 10^{-12}$ and $5 \times 10^{-9}$.
- Expected answers checked using: an independent script that never calls `solution.py` and evaluates the exact closed forms above with 50 significant digits (mpmath), after converting every float input exactly, so the direct formulas suffer no cancellation. Coefficients: exact values for trigonometric-polynomial forces; for $N = 3$ with samples $[1, 2, 4]$, $a_0 = 7/3$, $a_1 = -4/3$, $b_1 = -2/\sqrt{3}$; for $e^{\cos(t-0.4)}$, $a_0 = I_0(1)$, $a_k = 2I_k(1)\cos 0.4k$, $b_k = 2I_k(1)\sin 0.4k$ (modified Bessel functions; aliasing for $N = 49$ is below $10^{-60}$). The complete responses for $e^{\cos(t-0.4)}$ at $\omega = 2$, $2 + 5\times10^{-9}$ and $3 - 10^{-12}$ were cross-checked by direct ODE integration (scipy `solve_ivp`, DOP853, `rtol = atol = 1e-13`) to within $10^{-11}$.
- Numerical tolerances chosen because: the references are exact to double precision, and a correct double-precision method reaches about $10^{-13}$. `RTOL = 1e-9` is the accuracy stated in `problem.md` and leaves a margin of $10^4$, while the near-resonance failures above are at least $10^{-8}$ relative at $t = 200$. `ATOL = 1e-11` covers values that are exactly zero ($t = 0$, $S_0$, and coefficients below $10^{-11}$).
- Important assumptions: $N$ is odd, so the interpolant has no ambiguous Nyquist term, and $\mu$ is defined as that interpolant. $\omega > 0$, so the constant part of the force ($k = 0$) is never resonant. The tests cover a non-resonant $\omega$, exact resonance with the third and second harmonics, $\omega$ within $10^{-12}$ above and below a harmonic, $\omega$ $5 \times 10^{-9}$ from a harmonic, and the constant mode $k = 0$.
